Renderer process limit

Low-end device mode
Smaller caches and GPU buffers. Saves the most; scrolling and animations may get less smooth
Disable background networking
No browser-internal pre-connects or service pings (pages and extensions are unaffected)

Caps runaway pages. Heavy web apps can crash when they hit a low limit
